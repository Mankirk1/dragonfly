# Using the Dragonfly task bot

The bot runs our plan from the *Radar Detection Timeline* Sheet. You don't need to open the Sheet to keep it up to date: signing off in Discord fills it in for you.

## Once: link yourself

Type `/link` and pick your name as it's written in the plan. From then on your tasks come to you by DM.

## Every week

- **Saturday:** the bot posts what we finished last week and what to focus on this week, and DMs each of us our tasks. Under each task it shows what "done" means and the next step.
- **Wednesday:** a DM reminder of what you still have open.
- **Two days before a milestone:** a heads-up in the channel with what's still due.

## When you finish a task

1. Press **Done** under the task in your DMs (or type `/done`).
2. Say briefly what you did and, if you have one, paste a link to the evidence: a doc, a photo, a file in Drive.
3. That's it. The Sheet's TASK BOARD and WORK LOG are updated, and the channel sees it.

Pressed it by mistake? Press **Undo** within 10 minutes.

## Other things you can do

| Type | To |
| --- | --- |
| `/tasks` | See your open tasks |
| `/week` | See everything in a week and who has it |
| `/progress` | See how much of each week is done |
| `/status` | Mark a task In progress, Blocked or Postponed, with a note |
| `/assign` | Hand a task to someone else |
| `/help` | See this in Discord |

## Good to know

- You can change tasks you own, tasks for "All", and tasks with no owner yet. For anything else, ask the owner or an admin.
- The bot never deletes anything in the Sheet, and you can still edit the Sheet by hand. The bot picks up changes next time it reads it.
- Something wrong? Tell whoever runs the bot, with a screenshot of the message.
